# Roadmap — Beauty DACH

This roadmap supersedes earlier launch sequencing where it conflicts.

## Gate 0 — hardening

- Recoverable Stripe event lifecycle and notification retries.
- HTML-safe transactional email.
- Composite tenant relationship constraints and adversarial isolation tests.
- Dependency policy, audit, Dependabot, CodeQL, runtime env validation, and release migrations.
- Public-route hardening, verified-email guest consolidation, retention policy, alerts, and restore drill.

## Assisted beta

- Recruit 3–5 Berlin barbershop/beauty partners.
- Concierge import and setup; publish in under 15 minutes.
- Validate agenda, deposits, cancellation, reminders, mobile operation, and customer self-service through complete booking cycles.
- Charge Founder Plan after the 30-day pilot to validate willingness to pay.

## General availability

- 14-day self-service trial and existing €39/€79/€149 plans.
- SMS/WhatsApp transactional reminders, waitlist, review requests, and Google Business Profile booking links.
- Privacy-conscious activation, booking, payment, no-show, and retention metrics.

## Growth

- Rebooking and inactive-customer workflows, occupancy and protected-revenue insights, consented campaigns, accounting exports, and partner integrations.
- Evaluate POS/TSE, native mobile, paid discovery, and an AI receptionist only from measured demand; none is a launch dependency.
